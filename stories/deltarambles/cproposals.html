# The 18 (?) Paths of Kultiman Socialism

## HSR Path / DR Path (Idea #11) 1. Destruction / Rebirth - The Rebirth Faction is a small fringe, mostly-unserious faction of the Kultiman left that advocates for the use of abyssal and light magic to destroy Kultima so outworlders (people outside of Kultima), who are already communist, could help the survivors build communism better. They mostly point to the catacylsms that affect Kultima, and with the lightners as proof. 2. The Hunt / The Ultra Silvers - The Ultra Silvers Faction is a group of fervent anti-capitalists, anti-reactionary people that seeks to eliminate advocates of any markets, unjustified hierarchies, imperialism and colonialism through the use of force and intense magic use. Some are organized militias, some are disorganised mobs. 3. Erudition / Scientific Socialists - The 'Scientific Socialists' Faction is a group of erudites, scientists and other intellectuals that seek to question everything about communism and socialism through scientific methods. They are divided into Jomi supporters and sceptics. Most of the scientific state institutions and communities are Jomi-supporters with a small dissident community formed in opposing the norm. 4. Harmony / Unity - The 'Unity' Faction is a faction of thought that advocates for unity in action, diversity in thought across Kultima. They are what formed after the initial clashes involving transitionary socialism and a soft-coup of Dajovy by Jomi and Anastasia towards the Supreme Council of Architects. This thought is popular because of Jomi's various works, and is the most dominant left-unity thinking across all state institutions. 5. Nihility / Apathy - The followers of this path are people who are disinterested in any ideology and live to survive. This was popular pre-Jomi as the Preservation was waning and the Eternal Winter encroached upon Dajovy and Pebble Town. After Jomi, this path lost its popularity, but is relatively common in most lightners after they descended into the dark world. This path can lead to self-annihilation, but is nowadays quite uncommon. 6. Preservation / Left-Conservatism (Dominant) - The followers of this thought are resistant to changes of the status quo and are commonly indifferent to any ideology except when it threatens their position and livelihood. This can be seen in some workers and bureaucrats that originally opposed Jomi but were swept up in the revolution and are now quietly managing the state. They also advocate for more walls and defense against the abyssal monsters and the eternal winter. They are also a subsect dissident faction of the broader Preservation, opposing both the Architects' one state capitalism and Interastral Capitalism. **_Dissident Faction:_** - There used to be a faction that used to be this role called the 'libertarians'. They were massive during the pre-Eternal Winter Era, and supported free market laissez-faire capitalism. They however died out after the Eternal Winter and were replaced by 'liberals' that advocate for restricted capitalism due to the conditions following isolation from interastral markets. Then, following Jomi's descent, and Jomi's reconnection of Dajovy to different dark worlds, an oppressed minority of bosses, owners and capitalists called the 'neoliberals' advocate for the return of free-market capitalism with of course, protections for them from profit-loss to make sure they can do exploitative business in and beyond Kultima. They are secretive and are small due to the implementation of transitionary socialism, as well as the Ultra Silvers. 7. Abundance / Utopian Socialism - They are a notable faction of the Kultiman left that advocate for methods to abolish unjustified hierarchies, cooperate harmoniously across Kultima with no master above, no slave below and create a post-scarcity society. They also advocate for better technology to achieve longer and healthier lives. They are also supporters of mutual aid, worker co-ops, and federations from the person to the overarching community of self-managing communities. They somewhat advocate for automation, seeing it as a useful tool in pursuing abundance, and commonly decentralize and democratize it to the masses. The people that follow this are commonly artists, dissidents and some lightners that don't fully support Jomi and her state in a magical world. 8. Remembrance / Archivists - This is a neutral school of thought common across leftists that seek to document anything real about the past, present and potential futures. This means they constantly find ways to talk and discuss about pre-Eternal Winter Interastral Dajovy, Eternal Winter Dajovy, Jomi's Dajovy then, Kultima Era, as well as the countless local stories in the underworld like Pebble Town and Novyjlevysk / Nuevo Leon. They commonly speculate about the past, and use a bunch of historiographical methods to look into the past and re-tell their stories to the world's present. 9. Elation / Absurdists - These 'Absurdists' are a faction of philosophy involving some leftists who maximise freedom and happiness across everyone. They differentiate themselves from other followers of elation due to their emphasis of freedom and happiness for all, often at their own expense. They are ambivalent to automation, using it to do boring work like administration and management but dislike it when it is used in the creative arts. They're also opposed to restrictions and temperance but don't do anything about the people that advocate so, because they think it'd be more funny to keep them around. They are common among the utopian left but not fully. 10. Trailblaze / Tomorrow - The 'Tomorrow' faction is a group of explorers, thrillseekers and connectors seeking to go beyond the Eternal Winter and Kultima, seeking other dark worlds and supporting people along the way. They love making and giving choices, and despise predetermined endings. They love the journey and seek to find their meaning in it, even if there is no end. They can be found all across worlds, classes and backgrounds. 11. Propagation / Ultra-Collectivism - A small fringe subsect of the left that seeks to bring everyone to groups upon groups, that seek as much cooperation and unity from every member of their organisations. They all advance technology with the goal of creating a society where everything is done in groups, and everyone is connected and related to anyone in some way. They believe all should share one culture, one methodology and one goal in life. They believe everyone is equal and powerful as well. They all also follow the propagation of everything, from foods to art, no matter what. 12. Enigmata / Fictionologists - The exact opposite of Archivists, they seek to focus on the mythos, legends and fables of the world. They are less focused on what is real, and instead on what is compelling. They also sometimes indulge in misinformation and spreading falsehoods, as well as trickery and false identities to cover themselves in a shroud of uncertainty and to give the archivists and scientists reasons to keep recording and seeking knowledge, so that there is always entropy in the world. They are a subset of Absurdists that focus more on uncertainties and falsehoods as their life's elation. 13. Order / Totality - They are a small radical group of intellectuals, managers, organizers following the 'Unity' of Jomi, that seeks to categorize, organize, and label everything into one coherent order of information and understanding. They seek to impose an order of things, a consistency across methods, schedules and thought. They often have plans, devise predictions or calculate futures. While some of these aspects are common in other paths, this group is more focused on order than ideology, which means they dislike any changes, even if it only threatens their order in small ways. A small subsection secretly wish for Jomi to be the immortal benevolent ruler of all, and subtly encourage this, but the followers are mostly kept secret from Jomi due to her disagreement with this subsection. 14. Voracity / Hedonists - A small faction of the 'Absurdists' that seek to fulfill their elation by consuming everything that their primitive desires makes them, however insatiable it may be. They are restless, and are always devouring something or looking to devour something. They are willing to resort to any method to fulfill their individual desires, and commonly hyper-individualistic. They live their lives in total bliss of hyper consumerism until someone stops them or are stopped by their physical and material conditions. They commonly accumulate and feast, and are antagonistic to conservationists. 15. Equilibrium / The Centre - They are for the balance of everything, and seek peace and compassion among all individuals and groups through this balance. Synthesizing multiple ideas from multiple strains of thought to form a balance from ever-changing states of ideology. They prioritise balance and agreeability over principles which often gets them clashing with almost every radical which is why this group is broad but also committed to being uncommitted. 16. Permanence / Cyclists - Not to be confused with people who ride cycled vehicles. They are a fringe group of people who believe, through the use of magic and technology that an immortal society will exist. They said this is best done through rebirths, new beginnings and endings. They believe in groups, or individuals being preserved through the creation they bring to the world, causing their permanent mark upon it. While this path synthesizes and shares ideas with a lot of paths, they are focused on metaphysical beings and meaning of life and so are often focused on that than practicalities of life. 17. Beauty / Romanticists - They are a small group across factions that see meaning in everything and the inherent goodness in any material or abstract form. Contrary to 'Apathy', they are invested in every little event and ideas in their life. Though, this means they see everything in ideals and heroic examples, and potentially clash with more logical or negative paths. 18. Finality / The Seers - A small faction of people who dedicate themselves, using technology, magic and other ways, to predict the end of everything. They often create scripts for themselves and share it among the others. They believe that there is an end to anything in the universe and seek to use the knowledge of when and how to delay it as much as possible across civilization. ---
